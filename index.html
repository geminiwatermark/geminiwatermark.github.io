    What Is the Gemini Watermark?

    The Gemini watermark is a semi-transparent label added by Google's Gemini AI to images it generates. It typically appears in the bottom-right corner as a small icon or text marking the image as AI-generated. Many users search for "what is Gemini watermark" or "does Gemini watermark images" — the answer is yes, Google Gemini adds a visible AI watermark to every generated image.
