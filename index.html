    What Is the Gemini Watermark?

    The Gemini watermark is a semi-transparent label added by Google's Gemini AI to images it generates. It typically appears in the bottom-right corner as a small icon or text marking the image as AI-generated. This is part of Google's transparency efforts to help viewers identify AI content.

    While the watermark serves an important purpose, many users need clean versions of their own AI-generated images for drafts, mockups, presentations, and personal projects. Our tool helps you do this responsibly.
