1. Introduction

Gemini Watermark Remover ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our tool.

2. Image Processing & Data Handling

Our watermark removal tool is designed to process images directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Images you upload are not sent to our servers. All processing happens locally on your device.

We do not store, copy, or transmit your images. Once you close the page or clear your browser cache, any uploaded image data is permanently removed from your session.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by contacting us via email). We may collect anonymized usage data such as page views and browser type through standard web analytics to improve our service.

4. Cookies

We may use essential cookies required for the basic functioning of the website. No tracking cookies are used unless you consent. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though this may affect site functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with third parties. Our website may include links to external sites; we are not responsible for their privacy practices.
