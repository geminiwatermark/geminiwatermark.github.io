The Problem with Photoshop

Photoshop is a powerful tool, but it is overkill for a simple watermark removal task. Here are the main challenges non-designers face:

  • Cost: Adobe Photoshop requires a monthly subscription that can be hard to justify for occasional use
  • Complexity: The interface is overwhelming for beginners, with hundreds of tools and menus
  • Time: Learning to use Clone Stamp, Healing Brush, or Content-Aware Fill takes practice
  • Inconsistent results: Manual editing quality varies depending on your skill and patience
