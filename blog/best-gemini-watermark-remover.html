If you regularly work with Gemini-generated images, you have probably explored different ways to remove the watermark. From manual editing in Photoshop to dedicated online tools, the options vary widely in terms of speed, quality, cost, and privacy.

In this article, we compare the most popular methods for removing Gemini watermarks in 2026 to help you choose the best approach for your needs.

Method 1: Manual Editing in Photoshop

Photoshop offers powerful tools like Content-Aware Fill, Clone Stamp, and Healing Brush that can be used to remove watermarks. However, this approach has several drawbacks:

  • Cost: Photoshop requires a monthly subscription ($22.99/month)
  • Learning curve: Manual editing takes practice and skill
  • Time: Each image can take 5-15 minutes to edit properly
  • Quality: Results depend heavily on user skill level

Method 2: Manual Editing in GIMP (Free)

GIMP is a free alternative to Photoshop that can also be used for watermark removal. While it is cost-effective, it shares many of the same drawbacks — a steep learning curve and time-intensive workflow.
